# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

School students (grades 9–13), university undergraduates and independent innovators across Sri Lanka who want to compete in, or follow, the national IoT competition. Also teachers, parents, industry partners and delegates attending the finals. Most arrive on a phone from a link shared in the SLIoT WhatsApp channel, Facebook or a school group, often on mobile data.

## Product Purpose

The official site of the SLIoT Challenge, the annual IoT competition run by the Department of Computer Science & Engineering, University of Moratuwa, with SLT-MOBITEL and IESL. It announces each edition, explains the categories, timeline and rules, collects registrations and proposals, and showcases past editions. Success is more teams registering and submitting proposals, and every visitor knowing where to follow for announcements.

Between editions the site runs in a "coming soon" mode: one teaser screen for the next edition that sends visitors to the social channels.

## Brand Personality

Confident, tech-forward, anticipatory. Voice is direct and encouraging to young innovators, never corporate. The feeling is an event-launch poster: something big is coming and you want to be part of it. Emotional goals: excitement and belonging, backed by the credibility of a university-run national competition.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS or startup landing templates (feature-card grids, hero metrics, purple AI gradients).
- Corporate university notice-board pages: dense text, no energy.
- Loud gamer / esports neon overload that drowns the content.
- Emoji used as icons.

## Design Principles

1. The robot and the title carry the brand; everything else supports them.
2. Phone first: the essential message and the next action fit the first screen on a small phone.
3. One clear next step per screen (register, submit, or follow).
4. Motion builds anticipation but never hides content; everything is readable without it.
5. Keep the established identity (navy-to-green night gradient, Audiowide display type, electric green accent); refine it rather than reinvent it each year. Exception: the 2027 coming-soon page uses an aurora palette (abyss navy to mint, `cs-*` tokens in `tailwind.config.js`), Bebas Neue + Montserrat, and a frosted-glass card over a blurred mock of the full site.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: text contrast at least 4.5:1 (3:1 for large display text), visible keyboard focus, meaningful link labels for icon links. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: continuous motion stops and entrances become simple fades. Works from 320 px wide and on low-end Android phones.
